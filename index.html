05 / Insights

Perspectives on finance and business.

Practical observations and resources on financial information, business performance and operating systems.

Many growing businesses work with partial or inconsistent records. The starting point is a structured view of what exists, what is missing, and which decisions the current information can support. A practical clean-up focuses on completeness, consistent classification and a clearer picture of cash, receivables and key cost drivers.

Useful projections rest on clear assumptions, realistic working-capital needs and transparent cash-flow timing. Lenders look for coherent logic, sensitivity to key drivers and evidence of repayment capacity. The goal is a structured model that supports informed discussion of funding size, use of funds and repayment profile.
